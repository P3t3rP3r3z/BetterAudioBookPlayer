## Full description (max 4000)

Better Audiobook Player plays the audiobooks you already have. Pick a folder and your book is ready, with its
chapters and cover art. There's no account to create, no ads, no subscription, and nothing you listen to
leaves your phone.

PICK UP RIGHT WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
• Every book remembers its own position, speed and progress
• A gentle rewind when you resume, so you never lose the thread (more after long breaks)
• "Continue listening" puts your current book one tap away

A SLEEP TIMER THAT HAS YOUR BACK
• Pick 10 to 90 minutes, type any time up to 12 hours, or stop at the end of the chapter
• The volume fades out gently instead of cutting off
• Nodding off? Shake your phone near the end of the sleep timer to add 10 minutes
• Fell asleep anyway? The app marks where the timer started so you can jump back

BOOKMARKS WITH NOTES
• Save a spot with one tap, then give it a name and a note
• Search your bookmarks and notes
• Share a bookmark or export them all as a text file

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR WAY
• Grid or list view, sorting, search, and filters for in progress, not started and finished
• Edit titles, authors, narrators, publishers and notes
• Cover art from your folder or the audio files, or a generated cover when there isn't one
• Plays MP3, M4A, M4B, FLAC, OGG, Opus, AAC and WAV

LISTEN ANYWHERE
• Plays in the background with lock screen and notification controls
• Voice control: "Hey Google, play Frankenstein on Better Audiobook Player", on your phone or in Android Auto
• Switch between your phone speaker, headphones and Bluetooth from the player
• Pauses when your headphones disconnect

COMFORTABLE TO USE
• Light and dark themes, with optional colours from your wallpaper
• Speeds from 0.75× to 2×, and adjustable skip back and skip forward
• Works with TalkBack and large text sizes, in portrait and landscape

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The app doesn't use the internet. No analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your settings and bookshelf can be
included in your own Android backup so they follow you to a new phone.

Better Audiobook Player doesn't include or sell audiobooks. Use it with audiobook files you own, or with
free public-domain recordings such as those from LibriVox.
