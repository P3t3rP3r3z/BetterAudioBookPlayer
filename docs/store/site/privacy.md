# Privacy Policy

**Better Audiobook Player** by Aikipunk Creations
Effective 29 September 2026

## The short version

Better Audiobook Player doesn't collect, send or sell any information about you. It has no account, no ads,
no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't use the internet.

## What the app stores on your phone

To do its job, the app keeps the following on your phone only:

- The folders you choose to import, and the audio and cover image files in them
- Book details you enter or edit (title, author, narrator, publisher, year, notes)
- Your listening position, speed and progress for each book
- Bookmarks and bookmark notes
- Your settings (theme, skip lengths, sleep timer and so on)

Nothing here is sent to us or to anyone else. Removing a book or uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Permissions and sensors

- **Audio files / storage** — to play the audiobooks you choose and find them again after a restore.
- **Notifications** — to show playback controls while a book is playing.
- **Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock** — to keep playing when the screen is off.
- **Motion sensor** — only while the sleep timer is running with "Shake to extend" on, to notice a shake.
  Readings aren't stored.
- **Vibrate** — to confirm that a shake extended the sleep timer.

The app can only open folders you pick yourself in Android's folder picker.

## Backups and new phones

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include your settings and a snapshot of your bookshelf
(book details, positions, bookmarks and notes) in your Google backup or when moving to a new phone.
Google handles this backup under your Google account's settings and privacy terms. We can't see it. The
audio files themselves aren't backed up.

## Sharing

When you share or export bookmarks, the app hands them to the app or file location you pick. Nothing is
sent unless you choose to.

## Android Auto and other devices

If you use Android Auto, Bluetooth, or your phone's media controls, the book title, chapter and cover are
shown on those devices so you can control playback. This happens on your devices only.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
